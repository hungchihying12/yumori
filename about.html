THE STORY BEHIND THE DIARY

Small moments, little discoveries.

Yumori is designed to feel like a well-loved notebook: somewhere to save colours you enjoy, remember outfit ideas and keep pieces of inspiration together. There are no rules to follow and no perfect style to reach. Your pages can change as often as you do.

A note about your data

Yumori currently saves entries in your browser using local storage. It does not create an online account or sync automatically between devices. Avoid saving private or sensitive information, and use Settings if you want to clear this browser's Yumori data.
